I got into security the practical way. I spent two years as an IT apprentice at Lørenskog Municipality, looking after 1,000+ users across 30+ locations. That meant a lot of Active Directory, Intune, Entra ID and Microsoft 365, plus crawling under desks to fix switches and access points. I ended up top of the team in closed cases (104% of target at best), and somewhere along the way I realised I cared less about *fixing* things and more about figuring out how they break.

So now I'm studying cybersecurity full time. This spring I did the **National Cyber League** and finished 173rd in the US and first in my class, which was a lot more fun than I expected. Most of what you'll find here is CTF writeups, small tools and whatever I'm currently trying to understand.
